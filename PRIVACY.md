# Petty Court by Kaushik Immadisetty Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Petty Court is a skills-only plugin. It is a set of instructions that the AI assistant you are already using (for example ChatGPT or Codex) follows when you describe a dispute.

## What we collect

Nothing. Petty Court has no server, no database, no analytics and no network calls. The developer never receives, sees or stores your messages or the rulings.

## Where your data goes

Your messages are processed only by the AI assistant you are using, under that provider's privacy policy (for OpenAI products, see https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy).

## Children

Petty Court is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
